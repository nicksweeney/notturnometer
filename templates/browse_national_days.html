National days

Through the Night marks the national days of many EBU member countries with a whole night celebrating that country's musical heritage, mostly drawn from its national public broadcaster. These programmes often highlight composers and works that are relatively unfamiliar to listeners outside that country.

Here is a list of national day celebrations, anniversary celebrations for national broadcasters, and other country-specific episodes since 2012.
